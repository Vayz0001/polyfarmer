Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Short version: this website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, and the Polyfarmer software sends us nothing. The details are below.

This website

polyfarmer.xyz is a static page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, has no forms and loads nothing from other websites. Fonts and images are served from this site.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. To deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, Vercel processes technical data about each request, such as your IP address, browser and the page you asked for. We do not use this data to identify or profile visitors. Vercel's handling of it is described in the Vercel privacy policy.

Links to other services

Links to GitHub and Discord take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply there.

The Polyfarmer software

Polyfarmer runs on your own computer or server. There is no account with us and no telemetry, and we never receive your data, your keys or your trades. Your wallet key is stored encrypted on your machine.

To do its job, the software connects to:

  • Polymarket, to read markets and place and cancel your orders.
  • A Polygon network endpoint, either one you set or a public default, to check which kind of Polymarket wallet you use.
  • PolyRewards (polyrewards.fun), to download its public leaderboard and find your rank on your own machine. Your wallet address is never sent to it.

Those services have their own privacy policies.
